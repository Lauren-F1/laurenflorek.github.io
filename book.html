About the Book

Heartbreak is universal. It levels everyone — no matter how strong, how successful, or how self-aware. Making Lemonade is the book Lauren needed and couldn’t find: honest about how much it hurts, unwilling to rush you past it, and quietly certain that you will not only survive but become more yourself on the other side.

Part memoir, part survival guide, it moves through the real stages of a breakup — the hope, the aftermath, the detox, the closure that turns out to be an inside job — with humor, hard truths, and hooks you can actually hold onto when it’s three in the morning and nothing feels okay.

For anyone in the middle of losing something and finding themselves, this is a companion for the whole messy, bitter, sweet way through.
