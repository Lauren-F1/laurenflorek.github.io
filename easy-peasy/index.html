What it is

If this workbook is in your hands, something probably ended, and I'm sorry. Not the reflexive kind of sorry. The kind from someone who has been on the bathroom floor and knows the tile personally.

This is the companion to Making Lemonade. Nobody makes anything for sorting-out the “messy middle” part of a heartbreak, so I did.

It isn't therapy, it isn't homework, and it isn't a program that fixes you, because you are not broken and I'm not in the fixing business. It's a place to write the truth down where you can finally see it.

What's inside

Six movements, in the order the book moves.

  • Movement One: The Truth
  • Movement Two: The Cost
  • Movement Three: The Understanding
  • Movement Four: The Conditions
  • Movement Five: The Standards
  • Movement Six: The Becoming

Each movement gives you a short essay, a few questions doing real work, an exercise or two, a place to record evidence, and one small action for the next 24 hours. The questions are few on purpose. Each one has a job.

Each movement names its home chapters in Making Lemonade, so the book is there when you want the deep end. You don't need it open to do this work.

What you get

Two versions, one download.

A print version to write in by hand, and a fillable version you can type into on a computer or tablet. Both arrive the moment you buy.

There's no finishing on time. You can go slowly, skip anything too raw today, and come back to it in a month. There is no such thing as being behind.
