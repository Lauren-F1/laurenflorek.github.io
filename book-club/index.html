What it is

You loved Making Lemonade enough to want to talk about it with people you trust. That's exactly the right instinct, and also slightly terrifying, because heartbreak books have a way of turning book club into unplanned group therapy if nobody's steering.

This kit is the steering. It's a host guide for one night: what to do before, how to open, what to ask, what to serve, and how to end it so people leave feeling held instead of cracked open and abandoned in the driveway.

You don't need to be a therapist, a grief expert, or the most put together woman in the room. You need to read it once before the night and pick the parts that fit your group.

What's inside

Everything the host needs, nothing she doesn't.

  • A host checklist that's a short list, not a project plan
  • Group agreements to read aloud, so the night feels safe instead of heavy
  • The shape of the night, a loose rhythm from arrival to goodbye
  • Two icebreakers, plus discussion questions for all ten principles
  • The lemonade recipe, made together as guests arrive, with notes on keeping it and spiking it
  • A few easy food additions, for hosts who would rather not cook
  • Copy and paste invitations for text, email, and group chat
  • A closing ritual that ends the night with real weight, instead of everyone just gathering their purses
  • What to do if your group wants to meet again

Use it for one gathering of about two hours, or split it across two nights if your group would rather go slowly. The discussion pages are built to be glanced at while you're talking, not read aloud word for word.

What you get

One PDF, ready to print.

24 pages, print friendly, and just as usable from a tablet propped on the counter. It arrives the moment you buy.

Reading the book first helps, but not everyone in the room has to have finished it. The questions work for a group that's partway through.
