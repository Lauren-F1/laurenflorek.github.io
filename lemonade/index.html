Be an early reader

Making Lemonade comes out this year, and before it does, I am looking for a small group of readers to read it first.

Early readers get a free digital copy of the book a few weeks before it goes on sale, no strings attached. If it means something to you, I would love an honest review when it launches. Honest is the important word there. A real reaction helps other women find the book far more than a polite one does. But there is no obligation, and the copy is yours either way.

This book is for the woman who is still going to work, still answering the texts, still being fine in public, while something quieter is going on underneath. If that is you, or if it used to be, I would love to have you.
