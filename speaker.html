Meet Lauren

Lauren Florek is a certified executive, life, and mindset coach, a neurolinguistics practitioner, and the founder of New Mindset Foundation.

With a background in psychology and more than a decade of leadership experience, her work is rooted in a simple belief. Our humanity is realized through exploring ourselves, and in that exploration, we see the thread that connects us all. She challenges audiences to embrace curiosity and compassion, inviting them to see that in both our uniqueness and our struggles, we discover connection and strength.
