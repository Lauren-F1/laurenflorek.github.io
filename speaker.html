Lauren Florek brings humor, psychological insight, and candid storytelling to the experiences that shape who we become.

Meet Lauren

Lauren Florek is an author, executive and life coach, certified neurolinguistics practitioner, and the founder of New Mindset Foundation.

With a background in psychology and more than a decade of leadership experience, her work explores why human beings repeat patterns, lose trust in themselves, protect stories that no longer fit, and sometimes need life to fall apart before they can see themselves more clearly.

Lauren combines personal storytelling, behavioral insight, thoughtful reflection, and an unapologetic sense of humor to make complex emotional experiences feel understandable and shared. Her work is especially resonant with women navigating endings, reinvention, leadership, relationships, and identity, while remaining relevant to anyone who has ever had to adapt, begin again, or make meaning from something they never would have chosen.

She is the author of Making Lemonade, a deeply human exploration of heartbreak, healing, self-trust, and what becomes possible when we stop asking why something happened and begin deciding what we will do with it.

Author. Coach. Student of the human experience.

Signature Keynotes

Four talks, one body of work.

01

Making Lemonade

How We Make Meaning from What Hurts

Pain does not automatically make us wiser. What changes us is what we come to understand about ourselves and what we decide to do with that information.

Drawing from her book Making Lemonade, Lauren explores what happens when life hands us something we never would have chosen. Through personal storytelling, psychological insight, and humor, she examines the stories we build around pain, the difference between healing and performing “fine,” and how we can move forward without pretending the hard thing was good.

Audiences leave with a clearer understanding of how meaning influences identity, how unanswered questions keep us attached to the past, and how one honest choice can begin changing what happens next.

Best for: women's conferences, wellness events, author events, community organizations, retreats, universities, associations, grief and transition programs.

02

Under the Right Conditions

The Personal Ecology of Becoming

Nature does not confuse difference with deficiency. Human beings do it all the time.

Using the imagery and stories behind the “Under the Right Conditions” chapter of Making Lemonade, Lauren explores why there is no universal way to grow, lead, heal, love, or become. Some women bloom in visibility. Others need privacy. Some thrive through connection. Others need more space. The problem begins when we judge ourselves by conditions we were never designed to thrive in.

This talk helps audiences identify what allows them to expand, what causes them to disappear, and what they may need to change so their outer lives have room for the person growing within them.

Best for: women's leadership conferences, personal development events, professional associations, retreats, employee groups, education, midlife and reinvention audiences.

03

Devastated Attractively

What Women Lose When We Perform Fine

Women are expected to be ambitious but agreeable, confident but humble, honest but not disruptive, hurt but dignified, resilient but never visibly exhausted.

In this funny and uncomfortably recognizable keynote, Lauren examines the impossible standards women carry and the quiet self-abandonment that occurs when we become more committed to appearing fine than understanding what is true.

Audiences explore how perfection, performance, people-pleasing, and emotional concealment influence their choices, relationships, leadership, and sense of self. The goal is not to become less capable. It is to stop using capability as camouflage.

Best for: women's conferences, workplace women's groups, professional associations, health and wellness events, universities, caregiving organizations, and audiences navigating burnout.

04

For organizations

Human Before High-Performing

Self-Trust, Emotional Regulation, and Honest Leadership Under Pressure

High performance becomes difficult to sustain when people are depleted, emotionally activated, afraid to speak honestly, or disconnected from the meaning behind their work.

Lauren combines leadership experience, behavioral insight, and practical tools to help people recognize how pressure affects communication, decision-making, trust, and emotional regulation. This session explores how leaders can remain steady without becoming detached, communicate clearly without becoming rigid, and create conditions where people can perform without abandoning their humanity.

Best for: leadership conferences, companies, executive retreats, management programs, HR events, healthcare, professional services, and high-pressure teams.

Beyond the Keynote

Conversations that continue after the room clears.

Lauren's keynotes can be expanded into interactive workshops, breakout sessions, facilitated conversations, retreats, and leadership programs.

Each engagement is shaped around the audience and the moment they are navigating. Sessions can explore the personal experiences that change us, including heartbreak, identity, reinvention, relationships, self-trust, and meaning, or the human dynamics that shape organizations, including emotional regulation, communication, leadership, accountability, trust, and change.

The goal is not to give an audience a temporary motivational high. It is to give people language for something they have experienced, a new way to understand it, and a practical place to begin.

For women, communities, and personal growth

  • The Story Beneath the Story: Separating facts, pain, meaning, and identity
  • Radical Honesty Without Radical Self-Blame: Accountability that creates growth rather than shame
  • Rebuilding Self-Trust: Learning to hear and honor your own knowing again
  • Your Personal Ecology: Identifying the conditions under which you thrive
  • The Cost of Being Chosen: Worth, belonging, boundaries, and self-abandonment
  • The Next Version: Using what you know to become who you choose
  • Closure Is an Inside Job: Moving forward when the answers never arrive

For organizations and leadership teams

  • Human Before High-Performing: Emotional regulation and sustainable performance
  • Vertical Conversations: Conflict, communication, accountability, and repair
  • Leadership Identity and Inner Authority: Leading without performing a role
  • Trust Under Pressure: Communication and psychological safety in difficult environments
  • The Conditions for Thriving: How leaders shape the emotional ecology of a team
  • Patterns, Pressure, and Presence: Recognizing automatic responses before they make decisions for us
